Margherita pizza (makes two)

Dough: 500 g flour, 325 ml warm water, 7 g dried yeast, 10 g salt, a splash of olive oil.
Mix, knead for ten minutes and leave to rise for two hours.

Sauce: one tin of crushed tomatoes, a pinch of salt, a little garlic. Do not cook it.

Topping: fresh mozzarella, torn, and a handful of fresh basil after baking.

Bake on the hottest setting, on a preheated tray, for 8 to 10 minutes.
Pizza night is Friday. Buy two bottles of sparkling water.
