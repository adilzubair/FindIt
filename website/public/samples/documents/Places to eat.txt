Places to eat

Lisbon
- Custard tarts warm from the oven, near Largo do Camões. Go early.
- Grilled sardines in Alfama, any small place with tables outside.
- Time Out Market for a quick lunch if it rains.

Porto
- Francesinha at a café near the station. Huge, share one.
- Fresh fruit and cheese from Bolhão market for a picnic by the river.
- Coffee and a pastry at the old café on Rua de Santa Catarina.
