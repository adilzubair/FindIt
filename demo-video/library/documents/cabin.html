Arrival guide

Welcome, Sam! Here is everything you need for your stay from 7 to 11 February 2025.

Getting here

The last 4 km are on a gravel road that is ploughed every morning. Winter tyres are required. Park in the bay next to the green mailbox; the cabin is a two-minute walk along the lit path.

Key

The key is in the lock box to the left of the door. Your code is sent by text message on the day of arrival.

Heating and the fireplace

Floor heating is on when you arrive. Firewood is in the shed behind the cabin. Please keep the fireplace door closed when nobody is in the room.

Northern lights

The best view is from the jetty, away from the cabin lights. We send an aurora alert by text when activity is high, usually between 21:00 and 01:00.

Checking out

Check-out is at 11:00. Leave the key in the lock box and the dishwasher running.
