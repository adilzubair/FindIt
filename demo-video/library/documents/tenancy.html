Residential Tenancy Agreement

Apartment 4, 27 Alder Row, Dublin 8

1. Parties

This agreement is made on 1 September 2025 between Harbourside Lettings Limited, of 12 Quay Street, Dublin 2 (the "Landlord"), and Sam Rivera (the "Tenant").

2. The Property

The Landlord lets to the Tenant the two-bedroom apartment at Apartment 4, 27 Alder Row, Dublin 8, together with the fixtures, furniture and appliances listed in the attached inventory (the "Property").

3. Term

The tenancy begins on 1 September 2025 and continues for a fixed term of twelve (12) months, ending on 31 August 2026. After the fixed term it continues month to month until either party gives notice in accordance with clause 11.

4. Rent

The rent is €1,650 per calendar month, payable in advance on the first day of each month by standing order to the account notified by the Landlord in writing. Rent paid more than seven days late may incur a late payment charge of €25.

5. Deposit

On signing this agreement the Tenant pays a deposit of €1,650 (the "Deposit"), equal to one month's rent, as security for the performance of the Tenant's obligations.

The Landlord will return the Deposit to the Tenant within fourteen (14) days of the end of the tenancy, once the keys have been returned and the Property has been inspected. The Landlord may deduct only unpaid rent and the reasonable cost of repairing damage beyond normal wear and tear, and must explain any deduction in writing.

SR

6. Utilities and charges

The Tenant is responsible for electricity, gas, broadband and television licence charges for the duration of the tenancy. Water and building service charges are included in the rent.

7. Use of the Property

The Tenant will use the Property as a private home only, keep it clean and in good condition, and not make alterations, redecorate or fix items to the walls without the Landlord's written consent, which will not be unreasonably withheld.

8. Pets

One cat may be kept at the Property. The Tenant will repair or pay for any damage caused by the animal, and will have the carpets professionally cleaned at the end of the tenancy.

9. Repairs and access

The Landlord is responsible for the structure of the building, the heating and hot water system and the appliances supplied. The Tenant will report any repair needed promptly. The Landlord may enter the Property at reasonable times, on at least 24 hours' written notice, to inspect it or carry out repairs, except in an emergency.

10. Insurance

The Landlord insures the building and the Landlord's contents. The Tenant is advised to insure their own belongings.

11. Ending the tenancy

After the fixed term, the Tenant may end the tenancy by giving at least 28 days' written notice. The Landlord may end it only on the grounds and with the notice periods required by law.

SR

12. Condition at the end of the tenancy

At the end of the tenancy the Tenant will return all keys and leave the Property clean and in the same condition as at the start, apart from normal wear and tear, as recorded in the check-in inventory signed by both parties.

13. Notices

Notices under this agreement must be in writing and may be delivered by hand, sent by post, or sent by email to the addresses given by each party.

14. Entire agreement

This agreement, with the inventory, is the whole agreement between the parties.
