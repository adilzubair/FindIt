# Demo video credits

`findit-demo.mp4` and `findit-demo.webp` show the real FindIt app, captured with its `--render-ui` mode on a small demo library. Everything in that library is either licensed for reuse or made up for the demo.

## Photos

The photos are from the [COCO 2017](https://cocodataset.org/) validation set. Each one is licensed by its Flickr author under [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/). In the demo library they were renamed to camera-style names; the two demo videos (*Weekend at the coast*, *Safari day 3*) are slow pans over some of the same photos. Photos with people in them were left out.

## Voices, documents and the app

- The narration in the two videos and the voice memo was generated with the Windows text-to-speech voices Microsoft Zira and Microsoft David.
- The invoice, lease, budget, trip notes, recipe and packing list are fictional. Names and addresses in them are invented.
- The FindIt icon and interface are part of this repository (MIT).
