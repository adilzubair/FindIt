# Code of conduct

FindIt follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the project and its users. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing others' private information are not acceptable.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/adilzubair). Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially, and the maintainer may remove comments, commits or contributions, or ban contributors, that violate this code.
